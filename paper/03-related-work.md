# Related-work positioning

Three categories to cite, with the one-line differentiator for each. The four
differentiators are threaded through all three. Competition-aware accessibility,
not proximity or count. Multi-utility distribution-grid feasibility fusion. Honest
county heterogeneity with a mechanism. The replicated renter and multifamily
falsification. No pre-registration is claimed.

**National charging-equity studies.** Work measuring DCFC or charger access across
income, race, or disadvantaged-community lines, usually with proximity, station
counts, or per-capita station density at coarse spatial units. This paper differs
by using a competition-aware accessibility metric instead of proximity or count,
and by reporting county heterogeneity with a mechanism rather than a uniform
deficit, including a named counterexample.

**SFCA and 2SFCA accessibility literature.** The two-step floating catchment area
family and its variants, Gaussian decay, enhanced, and three-step, mostly applied
to healthcare and increasingly to charging. This paper differs by applying the
family to DCFC with explicit method and radius robustness, by adding a per-capita
diagnostic that isolates the competition term as the explanation, and by coupling
accessibility to a physical infrastructure supply constraint, which this literature
rarely does.

**EV siting with grid constraints.** Optimization and facility-siting work that
places chargers under distribution capacity or hosting-capacity limits. This paper
differs by fusing two utility feasibility sources, SCE DERiM and the SDG&E-derived
ICA, into an equity-weighted siting program, by treating unassessable territory
honestly rather than dropping it silently, and by showing the grid bounds
equitable siting differently across utilities.
