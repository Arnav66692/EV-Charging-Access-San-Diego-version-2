# Figures and tables, 7 items

Three are non-negotiable, marked below. Tight-space fallback at the end.

**Table 1 (non-negotiable). Headline bootstrap.** DCFC DAC/non-DAC access ratio,
point and 95 percent CI, three counties, with the San Diego 10-mile local-access
note. Point made, the gap is county-specific, not uniform.

**Table 2. Robustness sweep.** DAC ratio across G2SFCA, E2SFCA, 3SFCA and radii 4,
6, 10 miles, three counties. Point made, the headline survives reasonable method
and catchment choices, LA across both, SD at 4 to 6 not 10.

**Table 3. Falsification.** Renter Q4/Q1 and multifamily Q4/Q1 DCFC ratios with CI,
three counties. Point made, those groups are not disadvantaged anywhere, the
deficit is specific to the DAC supply-demand geography.

**Figure 1. Study area and access surface.** Three-county DCFC access choropleth by
block group with DAC overlay and county boundaries, derived outputs only. Point
made, the spatial setting and the per-county normalization. Links to the public
map.

**Figure 2 (non-negotiable, methodological keystone). Proximity versus
competition.** Panel A, raw nearest-distance and ports-in-catchment, DAC versus
non-DAC, favoring DAC in all three counties. Panel B, per-capita supply (the
diagnostic) reproducing the three ratios. Point made, proximity misses the gap, a
competition-aware view reproduces it, including the Orange reversal. Caption labels
Panel B a proxy.

**Figure 3. Grid feasibility gap.** DAC versus non-DAC feasible-tier share by
county with sweep-range bars. SD and LA negative, Orange positive. Point made, the
feasibility equity gap matches the access pattern, the within-SCE LA evidence.

**Figure 4 (non-negotiable). Optimizer contrast.** DAC ratio versus budget for
gap-closing and total-gain objectives, three counties, with the LA cap annotated
and the unassessable LADWP candidate count called out. Point made, equitable
siting can close San Diego, the grid and unassessable territory bound LA.

**Public map.** Referenced as a resource in Data and Code Availability, derived
outputs only. Not a figure.
